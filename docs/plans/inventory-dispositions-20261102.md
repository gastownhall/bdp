## Engineering gaps that archival does not complete

- Current provider-neutral obligation mapping and test-system rollout remain
  active engineering work. Preserve actual requests, principal/key bindings,
  timing, crashes and cleanup; private controls cannot supply expected answers.
- PR334 and landed PR339 have different postimages in all eight compared files.
  The old stronger endpoint-pin admission policy is not current Core law. A real
  batch process-crash witness remains distinct from the landed clean-reopen
  tests and must be carried into current reliability work.
- PR272's startup assembly was not incorporated. Preserve its small typed
  configuration, validation-before-listener/storage, readiness, secret handling
  and cleanup requirements without reviving the whole historical framework.
- Current optional full History adoption is separate from the narrow historical
  Update and terminal reads. Its exact-read, complete-or-refuse, authorization
  and retention obligations remain in Core.
- Revisit replication/changefeeds, retained Snapshot minima and publication races
  during Transfer. Preserve the existing applicable safety fences meanwhile.
- Existing owned-Link design needs reexamination. Preserve currently admitted
  invariants minimally; do not infer an expensive ownership redesign from a
  historical correction or recovery test.
- Preserve OSS delete/prune/purge workflows and map their exact retention and
  recoverability requirements before selecting stronger all-copy erasure.
